# Incident Escalation Policy

## P1
- Acknowledge within 10 minutes.
- Assign an Incident Commander within 10 minutes.
- Publish the first stakeholder update within 15 minutes.
- Continue updates every 20 minutes.

## P2
- Acknowledge within 30 minutes.
- Publish updates every 60 minutes.

Only the Incident Commander may approve a production rollback during a P1 incident.
