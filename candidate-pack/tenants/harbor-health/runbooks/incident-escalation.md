# Harbor Health Incident Escalation Policy

## P1
- Acknowledge within 8 minutes.
- Assign an Incident Lead within 10 minutes.
- Publish the first internal update within 12 minutes.
- Continue updates every 15 minutes.

## P2
- Acknowledge within 25 minutes.
- Continue updates every 45 minutes.

Production changes require the named duty manager or incident lead specified by the service runbook.
